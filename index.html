About the server

What is TASEWAKAI?

TASEWAKAI is a community project founded by Kazu. It was created for people who want to learn Japanese through real social interaction, not only through textbooks.

The server combines Japanese learning, gaming, culture, content creation, events, and international friendships. It is meant to feel like a community hub rather than a strict classroom.

「ただ勉強する場所ではなく、人と出会い、話し、成長する場所。」 Not only a place to study, but a place to meet people, speak, and grow.
