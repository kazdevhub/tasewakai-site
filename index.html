About the community

What is TASEWAKAI?

TASEWAKAI is a community project founded by Kazu, created to bring together people who enjoy Japanese language learning, gaming, culture, content creation, and friendly international conversation.

The server is not designed to feel like a strict classroom. It is more like a social hub: members can practice Japanese, talk about games, share creative work, join community moments, meet people from different countries, and grow together inside a respectful environment.

「この場所は、ただ勉強するためだけではない。人と出会い、話し、成長するための場所。」 This place is not only for studying. It is for meeting people, speaking, and growing.
