About the server

What is TASEWAKAI?

TASEWAKAI is a community project founded by Kazu. It was created for people who want to learn Japanese through real social interaction, not only through textbooks.

The server combines Japanese learning, gaming, culture, content creation, events, and international friendships. It feels like a community hub rather than a strict classroom.

「ただ勉強する場所ではなく、人と出会い、話し、成長する場所。」Not only a place to study, but a place to meet people, speak, and grow.

Region history

Hokkaido

北海道 / ほっかいどう

Hokkaido became formally developed as Japan's northern frontier during the Meiji period, while Ainu culture gives the island a much older story.

Culture

Ainu heritage, winter festivals, wide farming towns, ski culture, and calm northern nature.

Famous foods

Sapporo ramen, jingisukan, seafood bowls, dairy sweets, melon, soup curry.

Landmarks

Sapporo Snow Festival, Biei blue pond, Hakodate night view, Furano lavender fields, Shiretoko.

Fun fact

Hokkaido is Japan's largest prefecture by area and has some of the country's strongest seasonal scenery.

Important prefectures

Sapporo, Hakodate, Asahikawa, Otaru
